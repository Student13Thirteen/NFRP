# Five-minute deterministic demo

1. Run `bash nfrp doctor`.
2. Open the URL printed by `bash nfrp credentials`.
3. Sign in with the generated administrator account.
4. Open **Acquire → Tolls**.
5. Upload `examples/tolls/demo-tolls.csv`.
6. Explain the parser, validation and `PENDING` boundary.
7. Upload the same CSV again and show duplicate handling.
8. Review and confirm the proposed rows.
9. Open **Tolls** and **Cost center**.
10. Open **Settings → Company identity**, change a color or company name and show the result.

The demo uses no operational data and does not require Telegram, Nextcloud, Ollama or a public domain.
