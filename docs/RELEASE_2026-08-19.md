# Functional synchronization — 19 August 2026

This release moves the public NFRP reference implementation from the 17 August 2026 boundary to the code-level behavior of the operational transport ERP on 19 August 2026, while preserving the same strict public-data boundary. It builds on [the previous snapshot](RELEASE_2026-08-17.md), which stays valid for everything it already described.

## Included in this synchronization

### One maintenance module instead of two parallel ones

Until this release the operator had to decide, before typing anything, between a "simple maintenance card" and a "multi-line invoice/DDT": two entry forms, two registers and two vocabularies for what people call one thing.

- a single manual entry, `/maintenances/new`: the first cost row is already open, so a one-line repair stays a short form, while `Aggiungi riga` covers a full invoice or delivery note with per-row VAT, category, vehicle or warehouse destination, driver and odometer;
- a single register, `/maintenances`, which lists registered maintenance documents and the legacy maintenance cards created before the unification, with shared filters (text, state, vehicle, category, supplier, driver, PDF) and shared pagination;
- lease invoices stay out of that register: they belong to the leasing module;
- the module tab bar is reduced to `Manutenzioni`, `Da controllare` and `Importa manutenzioni da PDF`;
- `/maintenances/expenses` and `/maintenances/expenses/new` answer `307` towards the unified routes and keep their query string, so existing bookmarks and links continue to work;
- the read-only assistant answers on the same set: its maintenance summary and search cover documents and legacy cards together;
- legacy maintenance cards remain readable and editable; no new record of that kind is created by the interface, and nothing was migrated or rewritten.

### Accounting precision and driver attribution

- expense lines keep the unit price with the same thousandth precision already used by fuel entries (`unitPriceMilliEuro`), while taxable amount, VAT and totals stay in cents;
- one allocation of a split line carries its own driver, so a line shared between vehicles driven by different people is represented correctly;
- the automatic driver proposal uses the dated tractor-driver assignment valid at the document date, resolves a trailer through its assigned tractor and always remains editable;
- the manual decimal separator is the point, with up to three decimals on unit prices.

### Editing a maintenance after it was posted

- descriptions, codes, notes and the PDF of a confirmed document can be completed later, with an explicit versioned PDF replacement;
- posted amounts, quantities and allocations stay closed to editing: correcting the text of a document never reopens its accounting content.

### Navigation and wording

- above 940 px every sidebar group stays open and opens or closes independently, so each work area is one click away from the dashboard;
- below 940 px the sidebar is a drawer that keeps only the current group open; when closed it is hidden by CSS from the first paint and also receives `inert` after hydration, so its links never enter the tab order or the accessibility tree;
- only the open item carries `aria-current="page"`; the group that contains it is highlighted visually, so a screen reader announces one current destination;
- the toll domain has one primary name in the interface, `Pedaggi`, in the menu, the page title, the back links and the cost center; `autostrade` remains only where it really means the provider or the file;
- commands name the page they open, so the button label and the destination title match;
- the top bar turns the current-position indicator into a `Torna a ...` link when the open page is deeper than the area index.

### Trips

- `Importa bolle container` always opens the upload page; the pending-review banner remains the single entry point to the review queue, so the primary button never changes destination under the operator.

### Data model

- one additive migration, `20260818130000_expense_price_precision_driver`: it adds the thousandth unit price and the allocation driver, converts the existing unit prices without touching taxable amounts, VAT or totals, and backfills the driver only from unambiguous dated assignments.

## Public-data boundary

Unchanged from the previous release: the repository contains no production database, uploaded document, backup, `.env`, credential, private endpoint or company record. Names, plates, suppliers, fiscal identifiers and OCR fixtures used in public tests are synthetic. Internal AI handoffs, private UX audit artifacts and server-specific deployment notes remain outside the public project.

## What parity does not mean

This remains functional parity for the identified modules and workflows, not a claim that a public clone is the production deployment. The unified maintenance module was verified through tests, a production build and authenticated route checks in the operational edition; the public edition adds the same behavior with synthetic data. No route-by-route screenshot matrix and no formal operator usability study were produced for this release.
